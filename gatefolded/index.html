Gatefolded

Gatefolded is my company. It's public or private music sharing for artists, producers, labels, and anyone else who needs to get music in front of the right people.

Why I built it

In my consulting work, the same problem keeps coming up. Artists juggle a link-in-bio page, a streaming profile, a folder of private download links for demos, and a spreadsheet of who they sent what to. None of it tells them whether anyone actually listened. Gatefolded pulls that into one place.

What it does

  • A branded music page with in-browser playback, lyrics, credits, and links out to streaming and social platforms.
  • Private sharing for unreleased music, with password protection and email allowlists.
  • No account needed to listen. Labels, A&Rs, curators, and collaborators just open the link and press play.
  • Real analytics on who listened, not just who clicked.
  • Bonus content for physical releases. Print a password on a card in your vinyl or merch so buyers can stream or download extras.

Who it's for

  • Artists sending demos to labels or pitching to playlist curators and press, or who want a music-first page instead of a generic link-in-bio.
  • Producers sharing beats and works in progress with artists and collaborators.
  • Labels and managers sending advance music to press, partners, and their team.
