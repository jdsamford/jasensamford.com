Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work?

Most of the work happens over email. I'll collect the details of what's going on, ask clarifying questions, and put together a plan with clear next steps. When needed, I contact your distributor and DSPs directly. If you'd rather talk things through, I'm always happy to schedule a video call.

Who do you work with?

Independent artists, managers, small labels, and music platforms. Whether you're releasing your first single or managing a catalog of thousands of tracks, I can help.

What topics do you cover?
  • Distribution strategy and choosing the right distributor
  • Release planning and timeline
  • Metadata hygiene (ISRC, UPC, credits, splits)
  • Royalty setup, audits, and troubleshooting
  • Streaming fraud prevention and best practices
  • Catalog cleanup and conflict resolution
  • Team training for artist services and support
How much does it cost?

$175 opens your case. That covers collecting the details, creating a plan, and contacting your distributor and DSPs as needed.

If earnings have been withheld or locked, there's also a 15% success fee, which applies only if the funds are successfully recovered. If you're not sure whether I can help, send me a note first.

Do you offer ongoing work or just one-off help?

Both. Many people start with a single case to solve a specific problem. If you need ongoing support—like catalog audits, release rollouts, or team training—we can discuss a longer engagement.

Can we talk on a call?

Absolutely. Email is usually the easiest way to gather information, but I'm always happy to schedule a video call on request. Just mention it in your message or reply to our email thread.

Can you help with sync licensing or publishing?

My focus is on distribution, royalties, and metadata—the operational side of releasing music. For publishing deals, sync representation, or legal advice, I'd recommend working with a music attorney or publisher. I can point you in the right direction.

What's your background?

I spent almost ten years at DistroKid, helping build systems that serve millions of independent artists. I've worked on everything from artist support to fraud prevention to internal tooling. You can read more on the About page.

What is Gatefolded?

Gatefolded is my company. It's public or private music sharing for artists, producers, and labels: send demos and advance music as one private link, give fans a music-first page, and see who actually listened. Consulting clients and readers get 50% off.

Still have questions?

Feel free to send me an email. I'm happy to chat before you get started.
