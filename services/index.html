Core work

Disputes and recovery

What most of my cases look like.

Frozen or escrowed royalties

Earnings held during a review, dispute or account investigation. I find out why the funds are held, what the distributor needs, and get the case in front of the right people.

Account locks and terminations

Accounts locked, suspended or terminated, including after a hack or a fraud flag. I work out the reason and what it takes to get the earnings released.

False DMCA takedowns

Releases removed after a takedown notice that doesn't hold up. I help document your ownership and get the dispute to someone who can act on it.

Copyright and ownership claims

Conflicting claims on your recordings or releases. I help you put together the evidence and handle the claim with the distributor.

Fraudulent duplicate uploads

Someone else distributing your music under their name. I help get the duplicates reported and removed.

Payout holds

Withdrawals paused, delayed or stuck in review. I find out what triggered the hold and what will clear it.
